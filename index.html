Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you deploy staff after I place a request?

We typically deploy verified staff within 24–48 hours of receiving your request. For emergency healthcare needs in Chennai and Chengalpattu, we can often arrange same-day deployment. Contact us via WhatsApp for urgent requirements.

Are all your staff background-verified and trained?

Absolutely. Every professional in our network undergoes thorough identity verification, credential checks, and reference verification before being listed. Healthcare staff must provide their registration certificates and we verify them directly with the relevant councils.

What if I'm not satisfied with the staff provided?

We offer a free replacement guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, simply contact us and we will arrange a replacement professional within 48 hours at no additional cost. Your satisfaction is our priority.

Do you provide live-in staff or only day-duty?

We provide both! Live-in staff for round-the-clock care (particularly popular for caretakers, nurses, and domestic maids), as well as day-duty and night-duty options. We'll match the arrangement to your specific needs.

Which areas do you serve?

We operate across Tamil Nadu including Chennai, Chengalpattu, Kattankulathur, Tambaram, Velachery, OMR, Coimbatore, Madurai, Salem, Trichy, and Vellore. Contact us for specific district enquiries — we may be able to help even if your area isn't listed.

Is Sathish Manpower a registered company?

Yes, Sathish Manpower Private Limited is a fully registered company with GSTIN 33ABSCS1284Q1ZI, operating from Kattankulathur, Chengalpattu. We provide all necessary documentation for corporate and hospital clients.
