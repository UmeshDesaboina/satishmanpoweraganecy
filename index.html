Frequently Asked Questions

For Candidates
How quickly can you deploy staff after I place a request?

We typically deploy verified staff within 24–48 hours. For emergency healthcare needs in Chennai and Chengalpattu, we can often arrange same-day deployment. Contact us via WhatsApp for urgent requirements.

Are all your staff background-verified and trained?

Absolutely. Every professional undergoes thorough identity verification, credential checks, and reference verification before being listed. Healthcare staff must provide their registration certificates which we verify directly.

What if I'm not satisfied with the staff provided?

We offer a free replacement guarantee. If you're not satisfied for any reason, simply contact us and we will arrange a replacement professional within 48 hours at no additional cost.

Do you provide live-in staff or only day-duty?

We provide both! Live-in staff for round-the-clock care, as well as day-duty and night-duty options. We'll match the arrangement to your specific needs.

Is registration free for job seekers?

Yes, registration is completely free for all candidates. We never charge placement fees from job seekers. Simply fill out our application form and our team will reach out with matching opportunities.

How long does it take to get placed after registering?

For high-demand roles like nurses, caretakers, and security guards, placements can happen within 1–7 days. For corporate roles like HR or sales, it typically takes 1–3 weeks. We guide you through the entire process.

Is Sathish Manpower a registered company?

Yes, Sathish Manpower Private Limited is a fully registered company with GSTIN 33ABSCS1284Q1ZI, operating from Kattankulathur, Chengalpattu. We provide all necessary documentation for corporate and hospital clients.

Do you provide jobs outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes! While our primary base is Tamil Nadu, we have a growing PAN India placement network. We've placed candidates in cities like Bangalore, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and Delhi. Let us know your preferred location when registering.
